Search the teacher’s own words

A searchable corpus of the actual recordings — to ground notes and the concept map in real, citable passages. Only these recordings; not classical texts.

How citations work

Each result shows which session, the language, the speaker, and the moment in the session. The [[cite: …]] token is a reference you can paste into a note — click it to copy. When an online recording exists, the timestamp is a link that opens the video at that exact second. Audio-only sessions (Piriápolis) still resolve to the source line, just without a video link.
